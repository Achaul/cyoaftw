INTRO GUIDE AND ORIGIN REVIEW

Behavior
- The guide asks how you are, gives one response, and waits.
- Injury, feeling shaken, and needing space change the response and options.
- Asking for space starts no timer for another briefing.
- Known-topic answers and party offers no longer arrive back to back.
- Greetings and facts are authored. Optional AI can select an approved delivery by number only.
- Origins and opening scenes keep the selected facts, with no AI word-count expansion.

GUIDE EXAMPLE

Before (rattled):
"No shame in it. Take a breath. Rattled is only what alive feels like."
The same turn then includes the backstory briefing.

After:
Player: "A bit rattled, if I'm honest."
Guide: "We can leave the plans for a moment. Would you rather have some quiet, or talk about what to do?"
Player: "I need a moment."
Guide: "Take your time. I will be here when you want to talk."
[The guide waits. No automatic next line.]

ORIGINS (same selections before and after)

COMING OF AGE
Before: You grew up in your family's trade, and you learned to fight in the militia drills back home. You left because you wanted to see the world before the world settled you. Now you have come to this town with a few coins and a promise: an old friend of the family swore to see you properly started.
After: You learned to fight in the militia drills back home. You left your family's trade because you wanted to see more of the world before settling down. An old friend of your family has promised to help you get started here.

REVENGE
Before: You learned to fight in the militia drills back home, and for a long time that was enough. Then came the worst day of your life, when someone burned your home to the ground. Since then you have followed one trail: that of the Ashen Court. It has led you here, and so has an old comrade who shares your grudge.
After: You learned to fight in the militia drills back home. Someone burned your home to the ground, and the trail of the Ashen Court has brought you here. An old comrade who shares your grudge has come to help you follow it.

REDEMPTION
Before: You swore a vow of service as a child and never took it back, but that is not what you are remembered for. You left your comrades to die, and you have carried it ever since. Now you mean to repay what you owe, whatever it costs. Someone who knew you before the worst of it has offered to help you begin.
After: You swore a vow of service as a child and never took it back. You left your comrades to die. Now you mean to repay what you owe, whatever it costs. Someone who knew you before has offered to help you begin making amends.

SEARCH
Before: You grew up running errands for a street crew, but your thoughts are always elsewhere. Your younger sibling left for this town to find work and never wrote again. The last trail you could find points here, and a friend who also wants answers has come to help you follow it.
After: You grew up running errands for a street crew. Your younger sibling left for this town to find work and never wrote again. Their trail leads here. A friend of theirs is helping you search.

FORTUNE
Before: You grew up running errands for a street crew, and it was almost enough, until a moneylender called in your family's debt. Now you want to clear every debt you owe. A fellow chancer who got you into this, or out of the last mess, has come along to see how far it goes.
After: You grew up running errands for a street crew. When a moneylender called in your family's debt, you came here to clear every debt you owe. A partner from your earlier schemes has come to help you find work.

EXILE
Before: You grew up running errands for a street crew, until you were accused of a crime you did not commit. You cannot go home, and nobody, as long as you stay careful may come looking. You came here to be nobody for a while, and one friend who vouched for you has followed to see you through.
After: You grew up running errands for a street crew. You were accused of a crime you did not commit, and you cannot go home. You hope to stay unnoticed here. A friend who vouched for you has come to help you start again.

CALLING
Before: You swore a vow of service as a child and never took it back, and then came a dream that returned three nights running. You are afraid, and going anyway. It led you to this town, where someone who understands such signs has agreed to help you read it.
After: You swore a vow of service as a child and never took it back. Then came a dream that returned three nights running. You are afraid, and going anyway. You followed the sign here. A pilgrim who recognises such signs has offered to help you investigate.

OPENING SAMPLE

You are a lean human. You have black hair and green eyes. Unanswered questions tend to hold your attention.

You learned to pick locks. Your oldest friend left for this town to find work and never wrote again. Their trail leads here. A friend of theirs is helping you search.

Chimney smoke hangs over the roofs. Beyond the town, the ruins of Aldermere mark what the Long War left behind. You stand in the tavern. Heads turn as you arrive, curious more than kind or cruel. Here and there you pick out a few of your own kind. You came with one aim: find out what happened to your oldest friend.
